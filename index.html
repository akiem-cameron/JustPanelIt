FAQ

PRODUCTION QUESTIONS

Who do you work with?+
We provide trade-only backer panel and box fabrication for Ontario sign manufacturers.
Do you sell directly to end customers?+
The service is structured as a trade-only production resource for sign manufacturers rather than a consumer-facing signage service.
Why would a sign shop outsource fabrication?+
Outsourcing can provide an additional production option when internal fabrication capacity is constrained by workload, staffing, project volume or deadlines.
Do we keep the customer relationship?+
Yes. The sign manufacturer keeps the customer, project and installation while selected fabrication work is outsourced.
Do we have to outsource our entire production operation?+
No. The service is intended to supplement internal capacity. A sign shop can outsource specific fabrication work when it makes sense for a particular project or production period.
Can I submit a project for a quote?+
Yes. Use the quote request form to provide the project information available to you.
What information should I provide?+
Whatever relevant project information you currently have — drawings, specifications, quantities and project requirements.
